SYNZAPP PRIVACY POLICY

Synzapp is provided to organizations for their staff to use at work. This policy
explains what information the service handles, what Synzapp can and cannot see,
and who else is involved. It is written to be read rather than to be survived.

Where your employer provides Synzapp to you, your employer decides how it is
configured and how long records are kept. Some answers about your own
information therefore rest with them rather than with us. Where that is the
case, this policy says so.

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR WHAT

Your organization is the data controller for the information its people create
in Synzapp: their messages, meetings and work records. It decides who has
access, how long content is retained, and whether a readable compliance archive
is kept.

Synzapp is the data processor. We operate the service on your organization's
instructions and do not use its information for any purpose of our own.

Synzapp is the controller for the account information we need to run the
service, such as the contact details of the person who administers it.

2. WHAT INFORMATION IS HANDLED

To provide access:
- A mobile telephone number, and the one-time codes sent to it. There are no
  passwords, so there are none to store or leak.
- A name, role and department, as entered by your organization.
- The devices a person has registered, so that access can be withdrawn from a
  device that is lost or belongs to someone who has left.

Created through use:
- Messages, attachments and voice notes exchanged between colleagues.
- Meetings conducted through the interpreter, together with transcripts and
  summaries where those are enabled.
- Operational records: standard work routines, root cause investigations and
  improvement items.

Generated by operating the service:
- Records of security-relevant actions, such as signing in, registering a
  device, searching preserved records, and approving a deletion.
- Technical information needed to deliver and troubleshoot the service.

3. WHAT SYNZAPP CANNOT SEE

Messages between colleagues are encrypted on the sending device before they are
transmitted. They can be opened only by the devices intended to read them.
Synzapp does not hold a key that opens them, and no member of Synzapp staff can
read them.

This is a property of how the service is built rather than a promise about how
we behave. It cannot be relaxed by a change of policy, a request from us, or an
instruction to our staff.

Photographs, video, voice notes and documents are protected in the same way.

Announcements are different, and deliberately so. A notice sent by your
organization to its staff is kept as a company record rather than sealed like a
private message, because its purpose is to be produced later if somebody asks
whether people were told. Your organization decides how long these are kept, and
the record of who confirmed reading one outlives the notice itself.

Actions work the same way. When somebody turns a message into an action, so that
a named team can fix a problem and confirm it is done, that action is kept as a
company record rather than sealed like the message it came from. This is stated
on screen before the action is created. Your organization decides how long these
are kept, and the record of who raised it, who did the work and who verified it
outlives the description of the problem itself.

4. WHERE AN ORGANIZATION KEEPS A READABLE ARCHIVE

An organization with a legal or regulatory obligation to produce its own records
may enable a compliance archive. Where it does:

- A readable copy of messages is retained for that organization.
- The copy is protected by a key belonging to that organization, held in Google
  Cloud's key management service. Opening the archive requires both database
  access and permission to use that key, and every use is recorded by Google
  independently of Synzapp.
- Messages sent before the archive was enabled cannot be added to it
  retrospectively, and any export states this rather than implying the archive
  is complete.

An organization enabling this is choosing to operate a workplace record system.
Staff should be told, and Synzapp states it here as well.

5. WHO ELSE PROCESSES INFORMATION

Two companies process information on our behalf. There are no others.

Google Cloud provides the servers, database, file storage and key management
that run Synzapp. Information is stored in the United States.

OpenAI provides live interpretation. Where a meeting uses the interpreter, the
spoken audio of that meeting is transmitted to OpenAI for translation and the
result is returned. This applies only to meetings where interpretation is
switched on. It does not apply to messages, which OpenAI never receives.

We do not sell information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use
customer content to train models.

6. HOW LONG INFORMATION IS KEPT

Retention is set by your organization and can range from days to indefinitely.

Where a rule to remove old content exists, nothing is removed at the moment it
expires. Expired content enters a review queue, and an administrator at your
organization must approve its removal, a step that requires them to confirm
their identity again.

Where records must be preserved for a legal matter, your organization can apply
a hold. While a hold is in place nothing it covers can be removed, by any rule
or any administrator. Lifting a hold does not take effect immediately.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Because Synzapp is provided to you by your employer, requests concerning your
own information are usually directed to them, and they can act on those requests
within the service.

Where you cannot reach your employer, or your request concerns Synzapp itself,
contact us using the form on our website. We will respond, and where the request
properly belongs with your employer we will tell you so rather than leaving it
unanswered.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or
port your information, and to object to certain processing. Exercising them
usually begins with your employer.

8. HOW ACCESS IS PROTECTED

- Access is tied to a telephone number and a one-time code. There is no password
  to be guessed, reused or leaked.
- Actions that permanently destroy an organization's records require the
  administrator to confirm their identity again, so an unattended signed-in
  computer is not sufficient.
- Searching preserved records, producing them, approving a deletion and lifting
  a hold are each recorded with the identity of the person responsible.
- Synzapp staff have no route into customer conversations. The tools our support
  team uses show that an organization asked a question; they do not reach the
  organization's content.

9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Information is stored and processed in the United States. Where an organization
is established elsewhere, this constitutes an international transfer, and
appropriate safeguards apply between us and our processors.

10. SECURITY INCIDENTS

If information handled by Synzapp is compromised, we will notify affected
organizations without undue delay, describe what is known, and say what we are
doing about it. We will not delay notification in order to have a complete
account first.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Every version of this policy is retained, with the date it took effect and the
identity of the person who published it. Where a change materially affects an
organization, we tell that organization rather than relying on them to notice
the page has changed.

12. CONTACT

Use the contact form on our website. Where you are reporting a security concern,
say so in the subject line and it will be treated as urgent.
